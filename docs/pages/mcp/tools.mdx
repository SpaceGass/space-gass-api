title: What the assistant can do
description: The SPACE GASS tasks an AI assistant can carry out through the MCP server, with example prompts and the tools it uses for each
---

You never call tools yourself — you describe what you want and the assistant
picks the tool. This page lists the kinds of task it can carry out, with
prompts to try, and names the tools behind each so you recognise them when
your client asks you to approve a call.

A few things hold everywhere:

- **Reads never change anything.** Tools under *Reads* only return what the
  job stores or has computed.
- **Writes change the open job in memory**, not on disk — nothing is saved
  until you ask. Deletions are previewed first. Every write can also be run
  as a preview that reports what would change without changing it.
- **Values are in the job's units**, and lists of Ids use SPACE GASS list
  form, so "members 1-7, 10" means what it says in SPACE GASS.

## Jobs

Open, create, save and close `.sg` files. One job is open at a time; the
assistant always checks the job before doing anything else.

> *"Open the Portal Frame sample."* · *"Open C:\Jobs\Warehouse.sg."* ·
> *"Start a new job from C:\Templates\Office.sgbase."* · *"Save as
> Warehouse-v2.sg."*

**Reads:** `get_job_status` · **Writes:** `new_job` · `open_job` · `save_job` · `close_job`

Ask to *"switch the API to read-only"* and the assistant calls
`set_access_mode`: writes are refused until you switch back, and your
licence seat is released. It only changes this when you ask.

## Structure

Nodes, members, plates, member offsets, supports and master–slave
constraints — built by position, one call for a whole frame.

> *"Add a 6 m 310UB40 beam between nodes 3 and 4."* · *"Pin every node at
> ground level."* · *"Delete members 12 to 15 and anything left hanging."* ·
> *"Tell me everything about member 7."*

**Reads:** `read_structure` · `get_node_detail` · `get_member_detail` · `get_plate_detail`
**Writes:** `edit_structure` · `edit_restraints` · `edit_constraints` · `delete_structure`

## Sections and materials

Library sections and materials by name (the assistant tolerates "310 UB 40.4"
and "310ub40.4" alike), custom sections by their properties, and built-up
sections assembled from library parts and plain shapes.

> *"Change member 7 to a 250UC73."* · *"Add grade 350 steel."* ·
> *"Build a section from two 150x90x10 angles under the flange of a 310UB40
> with a 10 mm plate beneath, and show me."*

**Reads:** `read_properties` · `resolve_section` · `resolve_material` · `view_section`
**Writes:** `edit_properties` · `build_section`

## Loads

Load cases, combinations, groups and categories; node loads, prescribed
displacements, lumped masses, member point and distributed loads, prestress,
plate pressures, thermal loads and self weight.

> *"Add a 5 kN/m downward UDL to members 1 to 4 in load case 2."* ·
> *"Create combination 10 as 1.2 × case 1 + 1.5 × case 2."* · *"What loads
> are on member 3?"*

**Reads:** `read_load_cases` · `read_loads` · **Writes:** `edit_load_cases` · `edit_loads`

## Moving loads

Standard design vehicles (T44, M1600, HL-93 and others), moving patch loads,
travel paths and scenarios, then generation of the moving-load cases.

> *"Set up a T44 vehicle travelling along nodes 1 to 6 and generate the load
> cases."*

**Reads:** `read_moving_loads` · `resolve_vehicle` · **Writes:** `edit_moving_loads` · `generate_moving_loads`

## Filters and plate layers

Named filters (saved selections SPACE GASS can apply), plate cuts along
which forces are reported, and plate strips for slab design.

> *"Save a filter called Level 2 beams with members 20 to 45."* · *"Add a
> cut across plates 1 to 6 between nodes 30 and 36."*

**Reads:** `read_filters` · **Writes:** `edit_filters` · `edit_plate_cuts` · `edit_plate_strips`

## Analysis

Linear and non-linear static, buckling and dynamic (natural frequency)
analyses. The assistant starts the run, waits for it and reports warnings and
errors. Settings you pass are saved to the job.

> *"Run a linear static analysis for load cases 1 to 3."* · *"Run a
> buckling analysis for combination 10."* · *"Which load cases have results?"*

**Reads:** `read_analysis_settings` · `get_analysis_info` · `get_run_status`
**Writes:** `run_analysis` · `cancel_run`

## Steel member design

Design groups (chains of members with their effective lengths and flange
restraints), then checks and designs to AS 4100 or NZS 3404. A design can
replace the members' sections with the designed sizes and re-run the
analysis. Needs static results first.

> *"Set up a design group for members 12 to 15 with effective lengths of 1.0
> and check it to AS 4100."* · *"Design the roof beams and update the
> model."* · *"Which groups failed, and on what check?"*

**Reads:** `read_steel_members` · `read_steel_design_settings` · `get_steel_design_results`
**Writes:** `edit_steel_members` · `run_steel_member_check` · `run_steel_member_design`

## Results

Node displacements and reactions, the equilibrium check, member end and
intermediate forces, plate forces and stresses, buckling load factors,
natural frequencies and mass participation. Results are sorted and paged, so
"the governing rows" is one call.

> *"What are the maximum reactions, and which case governs?"* · *"The five
> members with the largest bending moment in combination 10."* · *"Is case 2
> in equilibrium?"* · *"First three buckling load factors."*

**Reads:** `get_node_results` · `get_reaction_summary` · `get_member_results` ·
`get_plate_results` · `get_buckling_results` · `get_dynamic_results`

## Inspect and export

The interactive 3D viewer (in any client that supports MCP Apps, such as
Claude Desktop) — click to select nodes, members and plates so the assistant
knows what "these" means — the engine's recent errors and warnings, and IFC
export.

> *"Show me the model in 3D."* · *"Any errors from that last run?"* ·
> *"Export the model to IFC in C:\Exports."*

**Reads:** `view_model` · `get_errors` · **Writes:** `export_model`

## Everything else

For what the tools above don't cover — job headings, settings, imports, any
of the 430+ operations in the [API Reference](/api) — the assistant looks
the endpoint up and calls it directly.

> *"Set the job title to Warehouse B and the engineer to J. Smith."* ·
> *"Import C:\Models\frame.txt."*

**Reads:** `list_api` · `search_api` · `describe_endpoint` · **Writes:** `call_api`
