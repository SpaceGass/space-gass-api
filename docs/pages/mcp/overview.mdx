The SPACE GASS MCP server lets an AI assistant such as **Claude Desktop** work
on your structural models — open a job, build or edit it, run analyses and
steel design, and read the results — by talking to SPACE GASS on your own PC.
It ships with SPACE GASS 14.5 and later, needs [API access](/licensing), and
your model never leaves your machine.

ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok and Claude in the browser (claude.ai) cannot connect to

## Get started

<Stepper>
1. **Start the SPACE GASS API**

   You need SPACE GASS **14.5 or later** with [API access](/licensing), opened
   at least once. Double-click the **SPACE GASS API** shortcut in the Start
   menu and leave it running — the assistant can't start it for you.

1. **Connect your AI app**

   **Claude Desktop** (recommended) — double-click the extension bundle in
   your SPACE GASS installation folder and click **Install**:

   ```text
   C:\Program Files\SPACE GASS 14.5\MCP\spacegass.mcpb
   ```

   If the tools don't show up in a new chat, fully quit Claude Desktop from
   the system tray and reopen it. Details and troubleshooting:
   [Claude Desktop](/mcp/claude-desktop).

   **Claude Code** — one command:

   ```bash
   claude mcp add --scope user spacegass -- "C:\Program Files\SPACE GASS 14.5\MCP\server\SpaceGassApi.Mcp.exe"
   ```

   **VS Code, Visual Studio, Copilot CLI, Codex CLI, Cursor, Windsurf** —
   each takes that same exe path in its own config; see
   [Other desktop clients](/mcp/other-clients).

1. **Ask**

   ```text
   Is a SPACE GASS job open?
   ```

   The assistant should answer from SPACE GASS (no job is open yet). Then try
   *"Open the Portal Frame sample and show me the model in 3D."* More in
   [Your first session](/mcp/first-session).
</Stepper>

## What you can do

- **Build and edit models** — nodes, members, plates, supports, sections and
  materials, in plain language ("add a 6 m 310UB40 beam between nodes 3 and 4")
- **Load them** — load cases and combinations, node and member loads, plate
  pressures, self weight, moving loads
- **Analyse and design** — linear, non-linear, buckling and dynamic analyses;
  AS 4100 and NZS 3404 steel member checks and designs
- **Read results** — displacements, reactions, member and plate forces,
  buckling load factors, natural frequencies, design utilisations
- **See it** — an interactive 3D viewer and section shape builder, in any
  client that supports MCP Apps (Claude Desktop does); select nodes, members
  and plates in the viewer and the assistant knows what you mean by "these"

Every kind of task, with prompts to try: [What the assistant can do](/mcp/tools).

## Privacy and security

- Runs on your PC and talks only to `localhost`. What leaves your machine is
  whatever your AI client sends to its own model — your prompts and the tool
  results it reads.
- No authentication; the API is reachable only on your machine, and access
  is gated by your SPACE GASS licence.
- The assistant can **change, delete and save** models. Deletions are
  previewed first and nothing reaches disk until a save, but keep backups,
  and ask the assistant to switch the API to read-only when you only want to
  explore.
