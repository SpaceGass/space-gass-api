Need help with the SPACE GASS API? Reach the team directly:

**Email:** [support@spacegass.com](mailto:support@spacegass.com)

When reporting an issue, please include:

- Your SPACE GASS Desktop version (visible at the top of the SPACE GASS window, or via `GET /service/info`)
- The endpoint you were calling and the request body, if applicable
- The full error response (status code, `title`, `detail`, and `traceId`)
- A short description of what you expected to happen

If the problem is with the [MCP server](/mcp), also include:

- The AI client and its version (Claude Desktop, VS Code, Claude Code, …)
- What you asked the assistant, and the error it reported
- Whether the SPACE GASS API service was running at the time
- For Claude Desktop, the relevant lines from
  `%AppData%\Claude\logs\mcp-server-spacegass.log` — see
  [MCP Troubleshooting](/mcp/troubleshooting)
